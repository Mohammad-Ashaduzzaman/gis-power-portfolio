01 — About

A GIS career built around applied spatial work.

This portfolio separates completed professional experience from utility-focused concept work. That distinction matters: credibility comes from showing what is proven and what is being actively developed.

My professional foundation is in Urban & Rural Planning, which naturally led me into spatial analysis, mapping, data management and decision-support. Since 2018, I have worked with GIS in institutional and project environments, including roles connected with WorldFish and UNDP Bangladesh.

Over time, my work expanded beyond desktop mapping into digital communication, interface thinking, web workflows and visual storytelling. I now use that combination to build GIS outputs that are not only technically correct, but also easier for engineers, managers and non-GIS users to understand.

My current specialization track is infrastructure and utility GIS—connecting assets, networks, land, risk, field evidence and planning workflows through practical spatial systems.

06 — Featured case-study format

What a serious utility portfolio project should show.

A map screenshot is not enough. Each case study should explain the question, data, method, result and operational value.

PORTFOLIO CASE STUDYTRANSMISSION GIS

230 kV Transmission Route Optimization

Illustrative decision-support workflow for comparing alternative transmission corridors while balancing engineering, social, environmental and land constraints.

ProblemIdentify a technically feasible corridor with lower ROW conflict and environmental exposure.
DataDEM, roads, rivers, settlements, wetlands, protected areas, existing grid assets, land-use and access.
MethodConstraint masks → standardized factors → weighted suitability → cost surface → least-cost alternatives.
OutputThree candidate corridors with length, risk, crossings and comparative suitability indicators.
ValueCreates a transparent, repeatable basis for early-stage engineering and field verification.
Portfolio integrity note: this is a portfolio case study using demonstration data. Route outputs support early-stage comparison only and require engineering, environmental/social, land and field validation.
